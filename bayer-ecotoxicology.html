Earlier research

Ecotoxicology Bioassays for Crop Protection

Environmental Safety division, Bayer CropScience, Monheim (2017)

Overview

Before an agrochemical reaches the market, its effects on non target organisms have to be tested under Good Laboratory Practice. As a trainee in the Environmental Safety division I worked on the laboratory tests that feed into these regulatory risk assessments.

What I did

  • Performed GLP compliant acute and chronic toxicity bioassays with soil dwelling model organisms, following standard test guidelines and SOPs.
  • Developed and presented an automated, image based evaluation workflow. Test plates were photographed and scored with predefined criteria for counting and endpoint assessment.
  • Documented work to GLP standards, including traceable records of test conditions and raw data.

Results

  • Completed a series of tests spanning acute and chronic endpoints.
  • The image based prototype reduced operator bias and turnaround time and improved data consistency.

Skills gained

The traineeship gave me hands on experience with regulated testing, quality documentation and process automation in an industrial setting, practices I have since applied in analytical method development.
